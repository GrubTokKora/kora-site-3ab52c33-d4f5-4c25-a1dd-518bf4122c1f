Delivery & Orders

Delivery questions, answered

Need help placing an order or have a question about our menu? Call us at (937) 280-4448.

Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Yes. Call (937) 280-4448 to order for pickup at 722 W National Rd. Delivery is available through Uber Eats.

What are your operating hours?

We are closed on Mondays. Tuesday to Friday we serve lunch 11:00 AM–2:30 PM and dinner 4:30–9:30 PM; Saturday and Sunday lunch is 11:30 AM–3:00 PM and dinner 4:30–9:30 PM.

Can I choose how spicy my food is?

Yes. Our curries can be made mild, medium or hot. Just tell your server, or mention it when you order by phone.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. Our menu has a full vegetarian section plus vegetarian starters and breads, and several dishes are vegan. Ask your server and we will guide you.

Where can I pick up my order?

Pickup orders can be collected directly at Curry and Grill, located at 722 W National Rd in Vandalia, OH 45377. Free parking is available right at our door.
