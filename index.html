About our restaurant

Modern flair, traditional recipes

At Curry And Grill, we pride ourselves on offering a unique dining experience that combines modern flair with traditional Indian recipes. Our menu is carefully crafted to bring you the best of Indian cuisine, from aromatic spices to rich flavors, creating a dining experience like no other.

We are a family-run restaurant on W National Rd. At lunch there is a buffet six days a week; at dinner you order from the full menu: kormas and tikka masala, goat kadai, biryani, and naan from the tandoor.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Call us at (937) 280-4448.

When is the lunch buffet?

Tuesday to Friday from 11:00 AM to 2:30 PM, and Saturday and Sunday from 11:30 AM to 3:00 PM. The buffet is $18.95 for adults and $12.95 for children.

What are your opening hours?

We are closed on Mondays. Tuesday to Friday we serve lunch 11:00 AM–2:30 PM and dinner 4:30–9:30 PM; Saturday and Sunday lunch is 11:30 AM–3:00 PM and dinner 4:30–9:30 PM.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Call us at (937) 280-4448 to book a table. For larger groups, please call ahead so we can set up your table.

Can I choose how spicy my food is?

Yes. Our curries can be made mild, medium or hot. Just tell your server, or mention it when you order by phone.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. Our menu has a full vegetarian section plus vegetarian starters and breads, and several dishes are vegan. Ask your server and we will guide you.

Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Yes. Call (937) 280-4448 to order for pickup at 722 W National Rd. Delivery is available through Uber Eats.

Do you serve alcohol?

Yes. We have a full bar with beer, wine and cocktails to go with your meal.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is a free parking lot at the restaurant, and the entrance, seating and restrooms are wheelchair accessible.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are available at the restaurant. Stop by or call (937) 280-4448.
